\# Week 2 - Data Validation



\## Project

EchoChain - Circular Economy \& Secondary Market Lifecycle



\## Week 2 Task

Data Validation and Quality Checks



\## Objective



After ingestion and cleaning, the data needs to be validated to ensure that it is suitable for downstream processing and analysis.



\## Validation Activities



\- Reviewed the processed data structure.

\- Checked the availability of required fields.

\- Reviewed data consistency after transformation.

\- Checked for potential missing or invalid values.

\- Confirmed that the processed data follows the expected workflow.



\## Validation Flow



Processed Data

&#x20;       ↓

Schema Check

&#x20;       ↓

Data Quality Check

&#x20;       ↓

Missing / Invalid Value Review

&#x20;       ↓

Validated Data



\## Outcome



The validation stage provides confidence that the processed data is structured correctly and ready for the next stage of the analytics workflow.
