\# Week 2 - Bronze Layer Validation



\## Project

EchoChain - Circular Economy \& Secondary Market Lifecycle



\## Objective



Validate the data stored in the Bronze layer after the ingestion process.



\## Validation Activities



\- Checked the ingested data structure.

\- Reviewed the Bronze-layer data for consistency.

\- Verified that the incoming records were available for further processing.

\- Checked the data before moving toward cleaning and transformation.



\## Data Quality Focus



The validation stage helps identify potential issues in the raw data before applying further transformations.



Key checks include:



\- Record availability

\- Column structure

\- Missing values

\- Data consistency

\- Readiness for transformation



\## Outcome



The Bronze-layer validation provided a basic quality check of the ingested data and prepared it for the next stage of the data pipeline.
